## Description (4000 chars max)

QicTrader is South Africa's leading peer-to-peer cryptocurrency exchange. Buy and sell Bitcoin, Ethereum, USDT and more directly with other people — no middlemen, no surprises.

**Why QicTrader?**

• **Real peer-to-peer** — trade directly with verified users at prices you agree on
• **Secure escrow** — crypto is held in escrow until both parties confirm payment
• **250+ payment methods** — EFT, cash, mobile money, and more
• **KYC-verified users** — identity verification builds trust across the marketplace
• **24/7 dispute resolution** — our team backs every trade

**Features**

• Multi-currency wallets: BTC, ETH, SOL, USDT, USDC
• Deposit and withdraw to any external address
• Create buy and sell offers with your own terms
• Real-time trade chat with end-to-end encryption
• Biometric login (Face ID / fingerprint)
• Push notifications for every trade update
• Full transaction history
• Affiliate program — earn commissions for referrals
• Dark mode support

**Safe & Regulated**

QicTrader operates under South African FSCA guidelines. All users complete identity verification before trading. Funds are protected by multi-signature escrow.
