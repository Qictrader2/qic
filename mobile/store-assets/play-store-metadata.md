## Data Safety (declare in Play Console)

**Data collected:**
- Name, email address (account registration)
- Phone number (optional 2FA)
- Photos/documents (KYC verification — encrypted, not shared with third parties beyond FSCA-required KYC providers)
- Financial info: transaction history (not shared)
- Device/app IDs: push token (FCM, for notifications only)

**Data NOT collected:**
- Location
- Contacts
- Browsing history
- SMS/call logs

**Encryption:** All data in transit via TLS 1.3. Tokens stored in Android Keystore.

QicTrader is South Africa's leading peer-to-peer cryptocurrency exchange. Buy and sell Bitcoin, Ethereum, USDT and more directly with other people — no middlemen, no surprises.

**Why QicTrader?**

✅ Real peer-to-peer — trade directly with verified users at prices you agree on
🔒 Secure escrow — crypto is locked until both parties confirm payment
💳 250+ payment methods — EFT, cash, mobile money, and more

• Multi-currency wallets: BTC, ETH, SOL, USDT, USDC
• Deposit and withdraw to any external address
• Create buy and sell offers with your own price and terms
• Real-time trade chat
• Fingerprint / Face biometric login
• Push notifications for every trade update
• Full transaction history
• Affiliate program — earn commissions for referrals
• Dark mode support

**Safe & Regulated**
QicTrader operates under South African FSCA guidelines. All users complete identity verification. Funds protected by multi-signature escrow.
