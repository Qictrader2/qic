## Expected behaviour
Sources: the card's Expected behaviour, its four checklist items and its Mandatory QA coverage. Also pack v5.2 I1 (glass: opaque commit steps and plates, no stacked glass), I2 item 11 and the I7 row for 969, qt-v5.css v5.2 overlays, glass v3.2.1 sections 2, 5c and 5d, and the 819 and 645 regressions, which are accepted requirements that this card must not change.

1. One customer dialog. On a customer page, every dialog in scope opens in the shared v5.2 dialog. At 640 px and wider it is a centred panel with a 20 px radius, --shadow-dialog and a 480 px default width. Below 640 px it is a bottom sheet with 20 px top corners, the 36x5 handle, a 90dvh cap and its own scroll. The scrim is --scrim with no backdrop blur. Tab and Shift+Tab cycle inside and wrap. Escape closes a dismissible dialog. On close, focus returns to the control that opened it, even when a second dialog was chained in between (Withdraw, then Transaction status, returns focus to the page's Withdraw button). The title is the accessible name. Two stacked dialogs (Deposit, then the 819 Minimum deposit notice) have distinct generated ids and each announces its own title.
2. Exceptions that keep accepted rules. The 819 Minimum deposit notice stays acknowledgement-only: Escape, an outside click and the missing close button all do nothing, and only OK closes it. While a money request is in flight (withdraw, transfer, lock), Escape, the scrim and the close button are inert, which matches the Cancel button that is already disabled while sending. They work again as soon as the request answers.
3. Money dialogs keep today's outcomes. Withdraw, send-to-user transfer, TransferModal, lock funds and deposit reach exactly the results they reach today, with the same requests, the same fields and the same messages. The held TRON quote id from Continue is the feeQuoteId that is submitted. A transport failure keeps the withdrawal idempotency key, so the user's retry sends the same idempotencyKey. A step-up challenge (authenticator code or the pilot's emailed code) resumes the same withdrawal, with the same address, amount, note and quote. A double click sends one request.
4. Opaque from the commit step. Rendered material: WithdrawModal is opaque from its first frame, because its first frame carries the commit button for accounts without step-up (glass 5d, single-step rule). TransferModal is glass on 'form' and opaque from 'confirm' through 'verify' and 'success'. LockFundsModal is glass on 'select' and opaque from 'confirm' through 'success'. Neither turns back to glass on Back. TransactionStatusModal, ConfirmDialog (including the trade-room confirmations) and the 819 notice are opaque from the first frame with role='alertdialog'. Deposit is a glass sheet, and its address and QR code sit on an opaque plate. A dialog opened over another dialog is paper, never glass on glass. Nothing on the customer surface uses backdrop-blur behind a commit-step panel.
5. Glass dialogs. ManageWalletModal (no mount site, kept and migrated), the three offer edit dialogs, UserSearchModal and CreateDirectOfferModal use the glass dialog and sheet (tb-dialog and ph-sheet). Customer dialogs already on ui/dialog.tsx (ReportUserModal, ReportModal, PublicProfileView, TradeActions, OfferPreviewModal, AddPaymentMethodModal, settings PaymentMethodsSection, DemandSignalModal, ConsentSettings and SessionTimeoutWarning) pick up the same style on customer pages without code changes. The hand-rolled customer dialogs KYCRequirementModal and the /offer/resell EscrowModal move onto the shared shell with their content and behaviour unchanged.
6. One toast. Every toast is one component. On the customer surface it is the opaque v5.2 pane: --action-subtle fill, 1 px white edge, --shadow-overlay, 16 px radius, --foreground text, a status icon in its status colour and a 44 px dismiss target. Success and info use role='status' in a polite live region. Error uses role='alert' in an assertive region. Warning uses role='status', as proposed and drawn by the design pack. The default lifetime is 6 seconds. Hovering or focusing a toast pauses the timer, and leaving resumes it with the remaining time. Entry is 200 ms ease-out (translateY 8 px plus opacity) and exit is 180 ms ease-in opacity. Under reduced motion it is opacity only, 150 ms linear, with no moving progress bar. Staff and public pages get the same timing, roles and pause, and keep today's dark look.
7. The silent messages appear. Copying the referral link shows 'Referral link copied!'. A failed copy shows 'Failed to copy link' as an alert. Submitting a report shows 'Report submitted successfully' with 'Our moderation team will review your report.', and a failure shows 'Failed to submit report' with the server message as an alert. Submitting a review shows 'Thank you for your feedback!'. A missing rating shows 'Please select a rating' as an alert. A failed submit shows the server message as an alert.
8. Toasts raised while a dialog is open (proposal for review). They render inside the open dialog's bottom band, as an opaque pane within the dialog rather than floating over it. They stay inside the focus trap, are announced and can be dismissed and paused. When the dialog closes, an unexpired toast moves to the page region. Placement above the phone bottom bar is 906-0c.
9. Unchanged. Staff and public dialogs (admin banned-words with its ConfirmDialog, moderator ConfirmDialogs, admin dialog.tsx dialogs), ReacceptanceModal and SessionConflictModal open, submit and close as before. ## Diagnosis
This card is a consolidation gap, not a single defect. It is part 2 of the 960 split. Everything below comes from reading the pinned snapshot (root 63ec4ecc, frontend 1ac41624, backend d3ee9b5e) and the local v5.2 pack sources. Nothing was built, run in a browser or reproduced on staging, and no shared environment or Trello card was touched. evidence-index.json lists no local sources or downloaded inputs, and the card has no comments or attachments. There was no earlier investigation for this card to reuse: the previous revision directory (90a75f37) holds inputs only.

Verified by source, by file:
1. Two dialog systems. components/ui/modal.tsx is a hand-rolled overlay. It has no focus trap and does not move focus in or return it. It hard-codes aria-labelledby='modal-title' and aria-describedby='modal-description', so two open modals share ids. Its backdrop is bg-black/60 with backdrop-blur-sm, Escape is a document keydown listener, and it calls console.log at module load and on every render. components/ui/dialog.tsx is the Radix wrapper (@radix-ui/react-dialog ^1.1.15). It already traps focus, closes on Escape, returns focus and generates unique ids, but it has no bottom-sheet variant, a bg-black/80 overlay and the April look.
2. Importers of ui/modal.tsx at this pin: 12, not 13. They are 11 feature files (wallet DepositModal, WithdrawModal, TransferModal, TransactionStatusModal, ManageWalletModal and LockFundsModal; offers EditTradeLimitModal, EditOfferRequirementsModal and EditOfferMarginModal; direct-trade UserSearchModal and CreateDirectOfferModal) plus components/ui/confirm-dialog.tsx, which imports './modal' relatively. ConfirmDialog is used by one customer file, features/trade/TradeSidebar.tsx (four trade-room confirmations at lines 415, 436, 455 and 488), and by four staff files: admin/banned-words/page.tsx, moderator/reports/ReportActionForm.tsx, moderator/DisputesTable.tsx and moderator/ReportsTable.tsx. No file imports both systems at this pin. The card's count of 13 most likely included one of these. The implementation must re-run the import grep, which is the authority.
3. The stacked-dialog collision is real. DepositModal renders a second Modal, the 819 minimum-deposit notice, on top of the deposit dialog (DepositModal.tsx:594-624). Both carry id 'modal-title'. The 819 notice is deliberately acknowledgement-only: hideCloseButton also turns off Escape and the outside click (comment at line 590).
4. ManageWalletModal has no mount site. It is exported from features/wallet/index.ts and imported nowhere else, so it cannot be exercised in a browser today.
5. State ownership. useWithdraw (hooks/api/use-wallet.ts:218-271) keeps the idempotency key in a hook-level ref. It reuses the key after a transport failure and clears it after a typed API answer. WithdrawModal calls useWithdraw, useTransfer, the held TRON quote (heldTronQuote, quoteSessionId, quoteHoldAttemptRef) and the step-up state (show2faPrompt, showEmailPrompt, the codes, pendingAction) in its own body, above the dialog. The wallet pages mount Deposit, Withdraw and Transfer unconditionally ((dashboard)/wallet/page.tsx:249-262 and wallet/manage/[currency]/page.tsx:576-598). LockFundsModal is mounted only while balance !== null (manage page line 600). TransferModal and LockFundsModal also keep their step state and mutations in their own bodies. Radix unmounts DialogContent children on close, which is the same as Modal returning null today. So the key, quote and step-up state survive as long as no hook or step state moves into a component rendered inside DialogContent. The migration must keep it that way.
6. Silent notifications. reports/ReportUserModal.tsx:23, profile/AffiliateDashboard.tsx:21 and features/trade/TradeReviewForm.tsx:14 import toast from 'sonner'. No <Toaster> is mounted anywhere in src (grep for Toaster and data-sonner finds none), so 'Report submitted successfully', 'Failed to submit report', 'Referral link copied!', 'Failed to copy link', 'Please select a rating', 'Thank you for your feedback!' and the review API error never render. This is a source finding, not a recorded reproduction. ReportUserModal passes sonner's {description} option on both success and error, but useToast().toast.error has no description parameter today. sonner ^2.0.7 is in package.json and in both lockfiles (bun.lock and package-lock.json). e2e/pages/report.page.ts:411 looks for sonner's DOM.
7. The one live toast system. ui/toast.tsx ToastContainer is mounted once in app/layout.tsx:106 and fed by store/ui-store.ts. About 100 files call useToast. The container returns null when empty, so there is no persistent live region. Every toast has role='alert' with aria-live='polite', which contradict each other. The default duration is 5000 ms and does not pause on hover or focus. The 500 ms translate-and-fade has no reduced-motion fallback. The look is a dark slate-900/95 pane with backdrop-blur-xl, which is glass, while v5.2 wants an opaque pane.
8. Hypothesis to confirm in a browser spike: toasts inside an open dialog. WithdrawModal, TransferModal, LockFundsModal and DepositModal raise validation and error toasts while their dialog is open, and ReportUserModal raises its success toast while open. The old Modal has no aria-hidden or pointer-events handling, so those toasts show and can be clicked today. A Radix modal dialog runs hideOthers (aria-hidden on every body child outside its portal) and sets pointer-events:none on body. ToastContainer lives in the layout tree, outside the dialog portal. So after migration, a toast raised inside an open dialog would very likely be hidden from screen readers and impossible to click or focus. That would also defeat the AC3 pause on focus. Glass v3.2.1 section 5c says a toast is 'never on a sheet or dialog: while a sheet is open a toast waits until it closes'. Holding error toasts until close would make today's in-dialog validation messages invisible, which the card's 'exactly today's outcomes' rule forbids. This needs a designed answer (see implementation step 3).
9. Tests that depend on the DOM placement. Radix portals DialogContent to document.body, outside React Testing Library's container. Five suites read container text or query it: regression/ticket-769-withdraw-modal-runtime (32 uses), ticket-9dl9xx7a-step-up-2fa (2), ticket-776-withdraw-fee-unavailable (12), ticket-839-withdraw-fee-rate-limited (8) and ticket-withdraw-fiat-864 (12). No test mocks ui/modal or ui/dialog. regression/ticket-645-wallet-manage-modals.test.ts is a static source test: it requires WithdrawModal.tsx to keep '<NetworkDropdown', the exact 'onChange={(symbol) => { setChosenRowSymbol(symbol)' shape, 'getVisibleWithdrawalCurrencies(solanaEnabled)' and the USDT rail objects, and the manage page to keep its setActiveTransactionModal calls.
10. Hand-rolled dialogs that use neither system. Customer: features/escrow/KYCRequirementModal.tsx (on /offer/[id] and /offer/create), app/(main)/offer/resell/_components/EscrowModal.tsx (on /offer/resell and /marketplace) and web3/EscrowConfirmModal.tsx (via web3/EscrowCard and EscrowList on /escrow, an excluded page). Excluded by owner decision (pack I3 and I9): auth TwoFactorLoginModal and EmailCodeLoginModal (card 904 keeps today's look), the chat image viewer in features/trade/ChatAttachment.tsx (not drawn) and /whatsapp. Staff: ModeratorActionModal, UserActionModals, RecoveryConfirmationModal and others. Global and outside the redesign: features/auth/SessionConflictModal.tsx (hand-rolled, its own Escape) and legal/ReacceptanceModal.tsx (uses DialogPrimitive directly with its own classes and blocks every dismiss path). Both are AC4 regression subjects, not migration targets.
11. Design sources read directly: pack-v5.2-index.md (I1 glass rule, I2 item 11, I7 card 969 row, I9 gaps), qt-v5.css v5.2 (.dialog, .sheet, .sheet-handle, --scrim rgba(15,23,42,.48) 'replaces bg-black/60 and the blur', --radius-sheet 20px, --shadow-dialog, --dur-sheet 280ms, --dur-exit 180ms) and glass-v3.1.css v3.2.1. From the glass file: .gl-dialog for tablet and desktop, .gl-sheet on phones with a 36x5 handle, .gl-solid for irreversible confirmations, section 5d (opaque from the commit step until the layer closes, never removed on Back, role='alertdialog', a single-step confirmation is solid from the first frame), 'never glass on glass', .gl-toast as an opaque --action-subtle pane with a white edge and --shadow-overlay, toast links in --action-pressed, toast motion of 200 ms ease-out translateY(8px) plus opacity in and 180 ms opacity out, and 150 ms linear opacity only under reduced motion. ## Risks
- Money dialogs (withdraw, transfer, lock). A careless migration that moves useWithdraw, the quote state or the step state into a component rendered inside DialogContent would remount it on every open or step change. That would drop the idempotency key (duplicate withdrawals on a retry after a timeout), the held TRON quote (a different fee submitted) or the step-up state. Mitigations: hooks stay in the component bodies, ticket-969-withdraw-state-survival.test.tsx proves the same key and quote across a transport failure and the step-up toggle, and AC2 is checked on staging with network logs. The change does not alter any request body.
- Toasts inside an open Radix dialog. hideOthers and body pointer-events:none would make in-dialog error toasts silent to screen readers and impossible to click. The in-dialog outlet fixes that, but it is a design proposal on top of glass 5c ('a toast waits until the sheet closes'), and Marcello or Christian must accept it at the design gate. Falling back to 5c literally would hide today's in-dialog validation messages and break 'exactly today's outcomes'.
- Test placement. Radix portals content to document.body, so five money-dialog suites that read container will fail until they read baseElement. The reviewer must check that only the query root changed and no assertion was weakened. ticket-645 is a source-string test and pins WithdrawModal's NetworkDropdown onChange shape.
- Staff surface. ConfirmDialog moves from the custom Modal to Radix on admin banned-words and three moderator screens. Their colours are unchanged, but they gain a focus trap, focus return and a portal. AC4 must exercise one of them end to end. Every default-surface class string in dialog.tsx and toast.tsx stays byte for byte, so admin dialogs built on dialog.tsx do not change look.
- Glass on real devices. The glass dialog and sheet use backdrop-filter, which was checked in Chromium only (pack I9). Safari and iOS need the -webkit- prefix (in 906-0a's port), and older phones may stutter. The reduced-transparency, data-glass='lite' and no-backdrop-filter fallbacks come from 906-0a and must be seen once on a dialog.
- Mobile sheet and keyboard. Withdraw and Transfer sheets hold inputs. On iOS the keyboard can cover the bottom band. The sheet uses a 90dvh cap and its own scroll, and the focused field must stay visible. Keyboard placement of fixed bars and toasts is 906-0c, so check it here only for the dialog's own buttons.
- Parallel work. 906-0c also edits ui/toast.tsx (placement), and both cards depend on 906-0a's globals.css port. Whichever merges second rebases and re-runs the toast tests.
- Dependency removal. Removing sonner regenerates two lockfiles. A lockfile regenerated by a different tool version can move unrelated packages, so the diff must touch only sonner entries. The frontend repo has no CI, so local tsc, lint, build and the targeted Jest suites are the merge proof.
- Ticket count discrepancy. The card says 13 importers of ui/modal.tsx, but the pinned source has 12 (11 feature files plus confirm-dialog). The implementation re-runs the grep and treats the result as authoritative. ManageWalletModal has no mount site, so its migration can be proven only by Jest.
- risk_areas tags money conservatively. No balance, fee, ledger or request changes, but the planned edits sit inside the withdraw, transfer and lock request paths, and a structural mistake there has a money consequence. No authorization, state-transition, migration or background-job behaviour changes.
