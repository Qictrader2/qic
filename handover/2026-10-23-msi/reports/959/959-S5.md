## Notes for S3 wiring
- Mount every step unconditionally. Drive `open` from the same allowed check that draws the opener.
- Release: pass `tradesApi.completeTrade`, or `completeTradeMutation.mutateAsync`.
- I've paid: pass `markPaidMutation.mutateAsync` or `tradesApi.markPaid`.
- Never pass the page's `handle*` wrappers; `send` must return the trade.
- Cancel: pass `() => tradesApi.cancelTrade(id)`, not `useCancelTrade().mutateAsync`. Its `onSuccess` replaces the cached trade with `{id, status}`.
- Proof upload: `uploadProofWithTradesApi(trade.id)`.
- Evidence upload: `tradesApi.uploadDisputeEvidence`, keeping the backend message on the thrown error.
- `onAdded` should refetch the evidence list.
