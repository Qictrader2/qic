2. Withdrawing = pending USDT withdrawal amount plus fee from GET /wallet/pending. Backend main never writes `wallets.pending_balance` for customer withdrawals (only admin paths call repo::wallet::update_balance), and `withdraw` debits amount plus fee in the same transaction that inserts the pending row.
